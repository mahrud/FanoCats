## $\mathrm{Bl}_2\PP^n$: the blow-up of $\PP^n$ at 2 points.
All varieties in this class have the same secondary fan, but as the multiplicity of the Cox degrees increases, the $\Theta$-collection expands, and with it the Ext table exhibits more complex behavior. Michalek proved in [[1009.0821]](http://arxiv.org/abs/1009.0821v3) that when $n>20$, such varieties do not have strongly exceptional collections of line bundles.
